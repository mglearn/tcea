# Annual Infrastructure Health Assessment

**Ardent ISD | 2026-27**

## Executive Summary

Ardent ISD's infrastructure is generally stable, but three areas require leadership attention during the next budget cycle: aging wireless infrastructure at two campuses, a growing concentration of student devices entering their fifth year of service, and a help desk backlog that spikes during August and January.

The district should prioritize a phased wireless refresh, protect the planned student-device replacement allocation, and adjust seasonal support capacity before adding new districtwide platforms.

## Evidence Review

### Network and Wi-Fi
- Internet utilization is not currently the district's primary bottleneck.
- North Ridge Middle School and Ardent High School show the highest density-related wireless complaints.
- Two MDF/IDF spaces need environmental and UPS lifecycle review before the next equipment refresh.
- Network monitoring should continue to distinguish internet capacity problems from local Wi-Fi, filtering, device, and application issues.

### Device Lifecycle
- 29% of the student fleet will be in year 5 by the end of the school year.
- 11% of staff devices are scheduled for replacement.
- Spare-pool levels vary by campus; two campuses operate below the district's 4% target.
- Equity review should consider not only device-to-student ratios, but age, condition, accessibility needs, and availability of loaner/spare devices.

### Help Desk and User Experience
- 82% of tickets met the current first-response target.
- Password/account, classroom display, and student-device issues represent the largest recurring categories.
- Ticket spikes align with school opening, semester transition, and major application changes.
- A seasonal staffing and communications plan should be tested before changing SLA targets.
