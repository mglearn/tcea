## Service Performance Bars

- First-response SLA: **86%**
- Reopen target performance: **95.2% stayed closed**
- Customer satisfaction: **88.4% of maximum score**

## Management Interpretation
The August surge remains the primary seasonal service challenge. September shows recovery, but wireless tickets at Ardent High School and North Ridge Middle School are moving in the opposite direction and should be treated as infrastructure evidence rather than isolated help desk issues.

## Next Actions
1. Build an August surge plan by March.
2. Publish password/account self-service guidance before the next semester transition.
3. Create a recurring-problem list for AV/display equipment.
4. Tag wireless tickets by building/area to support network validation.
5. Review SLA performance quarterly rather than changing targets after one difficult month.
