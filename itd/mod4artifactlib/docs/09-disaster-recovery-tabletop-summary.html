Disaster Recovery Tabletop Summary

Scenario findings, decisions, and corrective actions

Ardent ISD | Exercise Date: October 9, 2026

Scenario

A severe overnight storm causes an extended utility failure at the district administration site. The primary server room loses cooling after generator-supported systems remain online longer than expected. Several on-premises services become unavailable, while cloud services remain reachable from locations with internet access.

Exercise Objectives

  • Test leadership understanding of system dependencies.
  • Clarify decision authority and communications.
  • Identify gaps in recovery priorities and alternate work methods.
  • Verify that backup and restoration responsibilities are assigned.

Findings

What Worked

  • Staff quickly identified which cloud services remained usable.
  • Technology and facilities teams coordinated operational thresholds effectively.
  • The district's secondary internet path supported core administrative access.
  • Participants understood that business continuity decisions extend beyond the technology department.

Leadership Decisions

  1. Add environmental systems to the critical dependency map.
  2. Approve one districtwide recovery priority matrix with business-owner signoff.
  3. Conduct at least one restore-validation review each semester.
  4. Maintain a restricted, offline-accessible continuity contact list.
  5. Repeat the exercise with a different scenario next year.
