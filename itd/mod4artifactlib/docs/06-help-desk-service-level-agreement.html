Help Desk Service Level Agreement

Service expectations, priorities, escalation, and measurement

Ardent ISD Technology Services | 2026-27

Purpose

This SLA establishes shared expectations for technology support. It defines priority levels, target response times, escalation practices, and the measures used to evaluate service quality.

Service Hours

Standard staffed service hours are 7:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. on district business days. Critical incidents affecting districtwide operations may be escalated outside standard hours through designated leadership channels.

Priority Definitions

PriorityExample ConditionsTarget First ResponseTarget Communication
P1 - CriticalDistrictwide internet outage; SIS unavailable during critical operations; major VoIP outage; widespread authentication failure15 minutesInitial notice within 30 minutes; updates at least hourly while active

P3 - StandardIndividual device failure; classroom display issue; account problem with workaround available8 business hoursStatus update when assigned, changed, or resolved
P4 - RequestNew equipment setup; access request; routine change; consultation2 business daysExpected completion date communicated after review

Service Expectations

  • Tickets should include location, user, device/system, impact, and enough information to reproduce or understand the issue.
  • Priority is based on impact and urgency, not job title.
  • Tickets may be reclassified when scope or business impact changes.
  • Security incidents follow the district's incident response process rather than routine help desk workflow.

Escalation

  1. Technician owns initial triage.
  2. Team lead coordinates unresolved or cross-system issues.
  3. Manager escalates P1/P2 issues, vendor dependencies, or repeat failures.
  4. CTO is notified for districtwide operational impact, executive communication needs, or decisions involving significant risk/cost.

Service Metrics

  • first-response time by priority;
  • resolution time by category;
  • percentage meeting SLA target;
  • backlog age;
  • reopened tickets;
  • repeat incidents;
  • customer satisfaction;
  • seasonal ticket volume.

Review Cycle

Technology Services will review SLA performance quarterly and recommend changes based on evidence. Targets should not be lowered simply to make performance percentages appear stronger.
