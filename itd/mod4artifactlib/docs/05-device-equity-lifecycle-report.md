# Device Equity and Lifecycle Report

**Ardent ISD | 2026-27**

## Purpose

A 1:1 ratio can hide meaningful differences in device age, condition, repair access, and spare availability. This report compares campuses using multiple indicators so replacement and support decisions do not rely on a single districtwide ratio.

| Campus | Students | Assigned Student Devices | Ratio | Year 5+ | Spare Pool | Key Observation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ardent High School | 3,120 | 3,220 | 1.03 | 1,240 | 2.8% | Large aging cohort; spare pool below target. |
| North Ridge MS | 1,820 | 1,900 | 1.04 | 640 | 3.1% | Aging devices overlap with wireless concerns. |

## Leadership Findings
- Districtwide device availability is adequate.
- Ardent High School and Cedar Grove Elementary have the clearest combined lifecycle-and-spare risk.
- North Ridge's device lifecycle should be coordinated with its infrastructure improvement planning to avoid multiple disruptive changes at once.
- Replacement sequencing should include accessibility and specialized-program requirements before final quantities are set.

## Recommended Actions
1. Raise campus spare-pool minimum to 4% where practical.
2. Prioritize the oldest cohorts at AHS and Cedar Grove.
3. Review repair turnaround by campus before increasing total device purchases.
4. Align summer staging capacity with the number of devices scheduled for replacement.
5. Include device age and condition in board-facing lifecycle reports rather than reporting only the 1:1 ratio.

## Questions for Cabinet
- Is the district comfortable with variation in device age across campuses?
- Are replacement resources aligned to highest need, or simply distributed evenly?
- What instructional or accessibility needs require exceptions to the standard device model?
