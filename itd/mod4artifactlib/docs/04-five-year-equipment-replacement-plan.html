Five-Year Technology Equipment Replacement Plan

2026-27 through 2030-31

Ardent ISD | 2026-27 through 2030-31

Planning Principles

  • Replace based on risk, lifecycle, supportability, and operational need - not age alone.
  • Coordinate device and infrastructure replacement with district budget cycles.
  • Maintain inventory fields that support forecasting: purchase date, model, warranty, location, condition, replacement year, and criticality.
  • Revisit the plan annually as enrollment, pricing, support terms, and district priorities change.

Budget Strategy

The forecast should be treated as a rolling plan, not a promise of exact prices. Each year, the CTO should:

  1. update counts from verified inventory;
  2. review warranty and support dates;
  3. update enrollment and staffing assumptions;
  4. identify grant, E-Rate, bond, or local funding opportunities where applicable;
  5. review total lifecycle cost, including licenses, management, accessories, spares, implementation, and support.

Equity Check

Replacement decisions should examine whether campuses have materially different:

  • device age;
  • spare-pool levels;
  • repair turnaround;
  • wireless density;
  • accessibility requirements;
  • staff support capacity.
