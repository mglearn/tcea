## Purpose

This matrix helps district leadership compare vendor proposals using common criteria rather than purchase price alone. Technical scoring should be completed with qualified network staff; procurement and E-Rate requirements should be reviewed separately.

## Weighted Leadership Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical fit and scalability | 25% | Solution must meet present density and future growth assumptions. |
| Lifecycle cost | 20% | Licensing, maintenance, support, and replacement timing affect sustainability. |
| Implementation risk | 15% | School calendars, testing windows, facilities access, and cutover risk matter. |
| Supportability | 15% | District staff must be able to monitor, troubleshoot, and maintain the environment. |
| Interoperability | 10% | New infrastructure must integrate with existing switching, authentication, filtering, and monitoring. |
| Vendor support and handoff | 10% | Documentation, escalation, training, and post-install support reduce operational risk. |
| Procurement/E-Rate readiness | 5% | Proposal structure should support district review and documentation requirements. |

## Questions Before Selection
1. What assumptions are being made about cabling, electrical power, ceiling access, and MDF/IDF readiness?
2. Which recurring licenses or support costs begin after the initial term?
3. What equipment becomes district property, and what remains subscription-dependent?
4. What post-install validation will demonstrate that the design solved the documented problem?
5. What are the lead times, change-order conditions, and escalation paths?
6. What current E-Rate rules apply to each line item?

## Recommendation Process

Do not select solely from this matrix. Use it to identify finalists, resolve unanswered questions, validate technical claims, complete procurement review, and document the rationale for the final decision.
