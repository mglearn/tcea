Questions Before Selection

  1. What assumptions are being made about cabling, electrical power, ceiling access, and MDF/IDF readiness?
  2. Which recurring licenses or support costs begin after the initial term?
  3. What equipment becomes district property, and what remains subscription-dependent?
  4. What post-install validation will demonstrate that the design solved the documented problem?
  5. What are the lead times, change-order conditions, and escalation paths?
  6. What current E-Rate rules apply to each line item?

Recommendation Process

Do not select solely from this matrix. Use it to identify finalists, resolve unanswered questions, validate technical claims, complete procurement review, and document the rationale for the final decision.
