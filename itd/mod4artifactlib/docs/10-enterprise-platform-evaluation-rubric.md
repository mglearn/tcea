# Enterprise Platform Evaluation Rubric

**Ardent ISD | Leadership Review Template**

## Use

This rubric supports structured evaluation of districtwide platforms such as MDM, LMS, SSO, content management, curriculum management, and related enterprise services. It should complement - not replace - technical testing, privacy/security review, procurement review, accessibility review, and stakeholder input.

## Scoring Scale
**1 = Poor fit | 2 = Major concerns | 3 = Meets minimum | 4 = Strong | 5 = Excellent**

| Category | Weight | Questions to Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic and instructional fit | 15% | Does the platform solve a documented problem? Does it align with district priorities and existing workflows? |
| User experience | 10% | Can students, teachers, staff, and administrators use it consistently with reasonable support? |
| Interoperability | 15% | Does it integrate with identity, SIS, rostering, data, devices, and other required systems? |
| Privacy and security | 15% | What data is collected, shared, retained, protected, and deleted? What district review is required? |
| Accessibility and equity | 10% | Can all intended users access and use it, including users with accessibility or language needs? |
| Administration and support | 10% | What staffing, permissions, monitoring, training, and troubleshooting burden does it create? |
| Implementation and change readiness | 10% | What migration, training, communications, and adoption work is required? |
| Lifecycle / total cost | 10% | What are the recurring licenses, add-ons, implementation, support, and exit costs? |
| Vendor viability and support | 5% | What service commitments, escalation paths, documentation, and continuity protections exist? |

## Generative AI Use
A generative AI assistant may help draft comparison questions, summarize sanitized public vendor information, or prototype a scoring table. Do not enter student data, staff data, credentials, sensitive configurations, or vendor-confidential information into public AI tools. Human review is required.
