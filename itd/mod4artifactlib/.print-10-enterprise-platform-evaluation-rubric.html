Enterprise Platform Evaluation Rubric

Leadership rubric for MDM, LMS, SSO, content, and management platforms

Ardent ISD | Leadership Review Template

Use

This rubric supports structured evaluation of districtwide platforms such as MDM, LMS, SSO, content management, curriculum management, and related enterprise services. It should complement - not replace - technical testing, privacy/security review, procurement review, accessibility review, and stakeholder input.

Scoring Scale

1 = Poor fit | 2 = Major concerns | 3 = Meets minimum | 4 = Strong | 5 = Excellent

CategoryWeightQuestions to Ask
Strategic and instructional fit15%Does the platform solve a documented problem? Does it align with district priorities and existing workflows?
User experience10%Can students, teachers, staff, and administrators use it consistently with reasonable support?
Interoperability15%Does it integrate with identity, SIS, rostering, data, devices, and other required systems?
Privacy and security15%What data is collected, shared, retained, protected, and deleted? What district review is required?
Accessibility and equity10%Can all intended users access and use it, including users with accessibility or language needs?
Administration and support10%What staffing, permissions, monitoring, training, and troubleshooting burden does it create?
Implementation and change readiness10%What migration, training, communications, and adoption work is required?
Lifecycle / total cost10%What are the recurring licenses, add-ons, implementation, support, and exit costs?
Vendor viability and support5%What service commitments, escalation paths, documentation, and continuity protections exist?

Generative AI Use

A generative AI assistant may help draft comparison questions, summarize sanitized public vendor information, or prototype a scoring table. Do not enter student data, staff data, credentials, sensitive configurations, or vendor-confidential information into public AI tools. Human review is required.
