About the Portfolio

Leadership artifacts, not configuration manuals.

These examples focus on the decisions a K-12 CTO or Technology Director must be able to evaluate, explain, prioritize, and sustain.

How to Use These Examples

Each artifact can be viewed as HTML or downloaded as Markdown or PDF. Use them to study structure, identify the evidence behind recommendations, compare how technical information changes for leadership audiences, and adapt the ideas to local district processes.

Leadership Lens

  • What problem is being solved, and who experiences it?
  • What evidence supports the recommendation?
  • What systems and stakeholders depend on the decision?
  • What happens if the district does nothing?
  • How will the work be funded, supported, secured, and replaced?
  • What would success look like, and how will it be measured?
Responsible AI: Generative AI assistants can support drafting, summarization of anonymized data, question generation, and mock dashboard prototyping. Sensitive network details, credentials, student/staff data, incident information, and vendor-confidential materials should not be entered into public AI tools.
