## Communications Operating Rules

1. **One source of truth:** Incident Lead validates technical facts; Legal validates legal statements; Communications owns publication.
2. **Say what is known, unknown, and being done.** Avoid speculation.
3. **Use the least technical language that remains accurate.**
4. **Never publish security details that would create additional risk.**
5. **Set the next-update expectation.** Silence invites rumor.
6. **Keep versions.** Preserve every published statement and approval record.

### Media Q&A Guardrails

| Question | Recommended approach |
|---|---|
| Was this ransomware? | Answer only when confirmed. If unknown: “The investigation is ongoing; we are not characterizing the incident beyond verified facts.” |
| Was data stolen? | Distinguish “accessible/viewed/copied/exfiltrated” only when evidence supports the term. |
| Who attacked you? | Do not attribute unless law enforcement/forensics has validated and leadership authorizes. |
| Did you pay a ransom? | Refer to superintendent/legal; do not answer casually. |
| Why wasn't this prevented? | Explain layered risk management and current remediation; do not blame an employee/vendor before review. |
| How many people are affected? | Use validated count and clarify whether “potentially affected” or confirmed. |

## H. Parent FAQ - AISD-IR-2026-004 Sample

**Was my child's password exposed?**  
No. In this sample incident, the affected portal did not store district passwords.

**Was my child's Social Security number exposed?**  
No. The affected portal did not contain Social Security numbers.

**Why are you notifying me?**  
The district's investigation found that protected student information may have been viewed by an unauthorized person. Ardent ISD is providing notice consistent with its obligations and commitment to transparency.

**Do I need to freeze my child's credit?**  
Based on the sample data involved, the district is not recommending that step at this time. If the investigation changes the risk assessment, the district will update families.

**Why can't you tell us exactly who did it?**  
Attribution requires reliable evidence and may involve law enforcement. Publishing speculation can interfere with the investigation and create misinformation.
