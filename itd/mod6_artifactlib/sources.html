Sources and Legal Reference Points

These artifacts are training samples, not substitutes for localized board policy, district counsel, insurer requirements, or current state/federal guidance.

2026 Texas update: The library reflects the transition of statewide cybersecurity functions to the Texas Cyber Command and its current incident-reporting page. Texas law and agency processes can change; re-verify before operational use.

How the Library Is Grounded

The set treats cybersecurity as a district leadership risk-management function: governance, privacy, vendor review, incident response, communications, legal reporting, recovery, and continuous improvement. It uses the NIST CSF 2.0 functions as an organizing language and incorporates K-12-specific guidance from CISA, CoSN, K12 SIX, the U.S. Department of Education, TASB/TASA, and Texas statutes/agencies.

Texas Policy Alignment

TASB public materials identify CQB as the school-district policy area for cybersecurity, with recurring themes of a district cybersecurity plan, designated coordinator, training, and breach/incident notifications. Ardent ISD's samples cross-reference CQB rather than reproducing a TASB model policy. Districts should consult their localized Policy Online manual and current TASB guidance.
