# De-implementation Decision and Transition Plan: Software Portfolio Reduction

**Initiative:** Reduce redundant / low-value software subscriptions before the 2027-28 budget cycle.  
**Sponsor:** Deputy Superintendent  
**Project lead:** Chief Technology Officer  
**Current portfolio:** 43 paid instructional / operational tools reviewed in this cycle  
**Planning target:** Avoid or redirect at least $180,000 in annual recurring cost without reducing required services.

## Why De-implementation Is Needed

The district has accumulated overlapping tools through departmental purchases, grants, pilots, emergency implementations, and campus-level decisions. Some products have low utilization, duplicate existing capabilities, or create support and data-privacy overhead greater than their current value.

The goal is not simply to cut software. The goal is to create space for higher-value work and reduce unnecessary cost, risk, training burden, and fragmentation.

## Four Options

| Option | Definition | Use When |
|---|---|---|
| **Remove** | Stop the tool or practice completely | Low value; no required dependency; no replacement needed |
| **Reduce** | Limit licenses, campuses, users, or features | Value exists for a smaller population |
| **Re-engineer** | Keep the outcome but simplify how it is delivered | Workflow has too many steps or overlapping products |
| **Replace** | Move to a more effective or sustainable alternative | Required capability remains but current solution is not best fit |

## Candidate Review

| Tool / Service | Annual Cost | Active Use | Overlap | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Reading practice platform A | $76,000 | 22% of licensed students | High | Reduce | Retain for identified intervention campuses only |
| Staff survey platform B | $31,000 | 3 district surveys / yr | High | Replace | Existing district platform can meet need |
| Video creation tool C | $48,000 | 9% staff monthly active | Medium | Reduce | Move from districtwide to role-based licensing |
| Legacy file-transfer service D | $19,000 | Low | Low | Remove | Secure approved alternative already deployed |
| Classroom assessment tool E | $84,000 | High | Medium | Keep | Strong instructional use and curriculum alignment |

## Decision Criteria

Each candidate is reviewed against:

1. Required instructional / operational outcome.
2. Utilization and user dependence.
3. Student and staff impact.
4. Accessibility and equity.
5. Privacy / security / data-retention implications.
6. Contract term, renewal date, auto-renewal, and exit obligations.
7. Integration and data-export dependencies.
8. Training and support burden.
9. Availability and quality of alternatives.
10. Total recurring cost and cost avoided.

## Transition Plan

| Phase | Action | Owner | Due | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Confirm | Validate usage and contract data | CTO + Purchasing | Jan 22 | Portfolio review sheet |
| Decide | Approve keep / remove / reduce / replace decisions | Steering Team | Feb 12 | Decision record |
| Notify | Inform owners, principals, and affected users | Comms + Department Leads | Feb 26 | Communication log |
| Protect data | Export required records; confirm retention / deletion obligations | Data Governance + Vendor Owner | Mar 26 | Data disposition checklist |
| Prepare alternatives | Configure replacement / reduced license groups | Technology + Curriculum | Apr-May | Readiness checklist |
| Train | Provide targeted transition support | Instructional Tech | May-Jun | Training resources |
| Cut over | End use of removed / replaced tools | Project Leads | Jun 30 | Access disabled / new tool live |
| Close contracts | Complete non-renewal / termination steps | Purchasing | Contract deadline | Written confirmation |
| Review | Measure savings, service impact, and unintended effects | CTO | Sep 30 | Post-transition review |

## Stakeholder Communication

Communication should explain:

- What is changing and when.
- The reason for the decision, using evidence rather than blame.
- What users will do instead.
- What happens to existing content / data.
- Where training and support will be available.
- Who can raise an unresolved dependency before cutover.

## Success Measures

- At least $180,000 in recurring annual cost avoided or redirected.
- 100% of required data exported, retained, transferred, or deleted according to district requirements.
- No critical workflow interruption at cutover.
- No removed subscription renews accidentally.
- Support tickets related to de-implementation fall below 2% of weekly volume within four weeks.
- Stakeholder review confirms required outcomes are still being met.

## Post-Transition Questions

1. Did we save the expected time or money?
2. Did service quality stay the same or improve?
3. What unintended consequences appeared?
4. Did the replacement create a new burden elsewhere?
5. Should the district scale this de-implementation approach to another category?
