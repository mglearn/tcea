Project Management + Change Leadership

Ardent ISD Artifact Library

Fourteen classroom-ready sample documents show how a K-12 technology leader can define work, assign ownership, manage risk, communicate progress, support adoption, and close the loop after implementation.

How to use the samples: Open any artifact in the browser, download the Markdown source for adaptation, or use the print-ready PDF. The first 12 form the core project-management library; two bonus samples extend the collection into adoption support and de-implementation.
