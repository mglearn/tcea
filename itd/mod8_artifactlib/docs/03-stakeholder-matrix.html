Stakeholder Analysis and Engagement Matrix: Staff MFA Rollout

Project: Staff MFA and Identity Security Rollout
Owner: Chief Technology Officer
Review cadence: Every two weeks through enforcement, then monthly through closeout

Engagement Principles

  • Anyone whose daily work changes because of the project is a stakeholder.
  • High-influence groups need early involvement, not late notification.
  • Resistance should be diagnosed as information, motivation, skill, access, or workflow barriers rather than treated as a single problem.
  • Communications should identify what is changing, why, when, what the person must do, and where support is available.

Stakeholder Watch List

Signal What It May Mean Response
Campus enrollment stalls below 70% two weeks before deadline Awareness or desire gap; principal support needed Meet with principal; identify barriers; add enrollment session
Repeated concern about personal phones Access / policy concern Reinforce alternate-factor option; coordinate HR language
Help desk reports inconsistent recovery decisions Knowledge / process gap Refresh script, centralize exceptions, review identity-verification procedure
Vendor cannot support MFA Technical dependency Document system risk; consider compensating controls or access redesign
