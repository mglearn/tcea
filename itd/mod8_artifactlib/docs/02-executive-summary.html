Executive Summary: 2027 Student Device Refresh

Prepared for: Superintendent's Cabinet
Prepared by: Chief Technology Officer
Date: October 12, 2026
Decision requested: Authorize procurement planning for the 2027 refresh.

Recommendation

Replace approximately 2,600 student devices reaching the district's support threshold in summer 2027 and begin a rolling five-year refresh schedule. The current planning estimate is $1.56 million.

Why This Is Necessary

Ardent ISD supports approximately 8,400 students across 11 campuses. A large portion of the fleet was acquired during 2020-2022. The devices scheduled for replacement now show higher repair demand, battery degradation, and slower performance than the newer fleet.

Indicator Current Evidence
Devices scheduled to age out by Aug. 2027 2,600
Repair tickets involving affected models 31% above fleet average
Batteries below district health threshold 18% of sampled units
Campuses reporting testing concerns 7 of 11

If We Do Nothing

  • Repair and battery failures will continue to rise.
  • Campuses may require unplanned mid-year purchases at less favorable pricing.
  • Online assessment and digital curriculum access will become less predictable.
  • IT support time will shift from planned lifecycle work to break/fix response.
  • Equipment age will become less equitable across campuses.

Proposed Approach

Validate final inventory by December 2026; complete procurement during the winter window; receive and stage equipment in spring/summer 2027; deploy before the first instructional day; then retire and dispose of replaced devices according to district procedures.

Cost Component Estimate
2,600 devices @ avg. $540 $1,404,000
Cases, adapters, spares $78,000
Deployment / asset materials $18,000
Contingency $60,000
Total planning estimate $1,560,000

Success Measures

  • 100% of designated devices deployed before the first instructional day.
  • 100% recorded in asset management before campus delivery.
  • Less than 2% day-one deployment defects.
  • At least 95% of replaced equipment removed from active inventory within 45 days.
  • Aging-device hardware tickets decline at least 25% during the first semester.
  • A funded five-year replacement forecast is incorporated into the 2027-28 technology budget.

Decision Requested

Authorize Technology to finalize specifications, coordinate procurement with Finance, and return with final pricing and award recommendation before purchase.
