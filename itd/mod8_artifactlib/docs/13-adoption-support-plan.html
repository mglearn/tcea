Adoption, Training, and Support Plan: Staff MFA Rollout

Purpose: Ensure technical deployment produces confident, sustained use rather than one-time enrollment.

Required User Behaviors

By enforcement, staff should be able to:

  1. Complete MFA enrollment using an approved factor.
  2. Successfully sign in from a normal work device.
  3. Recognize and respond to an MFA prompt they did not initiate.
  4. Use the documented recovery process when a factor is unavailable.
  5. Know where to request an alternate authentication method.

Campus Champion Expectations

Campus champions are not expected to troubleshoot account-security problems. Their role is to:

  • Remind staff of deadlines.
  • Direct staff to approved instructions and support.
  • Surface common barriers to the project team.
  • Share campus progress with the principal.
  • Reinforce that alternate-factor needs can be handled through the documented process.

Support Model During Enforcement Week

Support Layer Responsibility
Self-service Quick guide, video, FAQ, known-issues page
Tier 1 Enrollment help, standard recovery, device-factor questions
Tier 2 Complex identity issues, legacy app conflicts, exception review
Leadership escalation Widespread campus disruption, policy / HR issue, enforcement decision

Accessibility and Access Considerations

  • Provide instructions in accessible digital formats.
  • Ensure videos include captions.
  • Offer a non-smartphone authentication option.
  • Provide in-person assistance for staff who do not routinely work at a computer.
  • Avoid requiring staff to disclose personal-device circumstances to supervisors; route requests through the approved support process.

Reinforcement Plan

30 days after enforcement:

  • Review adoption by employee type and campus.
  • Review support-ticket themes.
  • Close or revalidate temporary exceptions.
  • Add MFA to new-hire onboarding.
  • Update quick guides based on common user errors.
  • Share one short "MFA after launch" reminder focused on unexpected prompts and recovery.

Adoption KPIs

KPI Target
Voluntary enrollment before enforcement >=98%
Staff successfully completing first post-enforcement login >=97% without Tier 2 support
Approved alternate-factor requests fulfilled by deadline 100%
Staff rating instructions clear / very clear >=80%
MFA-related tickets after 4 weeks <5% of weekly help desk volume
