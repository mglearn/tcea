Critical Dependencies

School calendar

  • State testing windows restrict large device collections in spring.
  • Summer staffing is limited; staging must begin before staff vacations reduce capacity.
  • Campus deployment must finish before teacher workdays and student registration events.

Procurement

  • Competitive purchasing timelines may change the award date.
  • Board approval may be required depending on final purchase amount and district procedure.

Technical

  • Device management licensing must be available before staging.
  • Wireless and content-filtering profiles must be tested on the selected model.

Facilities / Logistics

  • Secure staging space must be available for at least 10 weeks.
  • Freight delivery must be scheduled around summer campus closures.

Weekly Project Review Questions

  1. What moved since last week?
  2. Which milestone is at risk?
  3. Which dependency is now on the critical path?
  4. What decision is needed, by whom, and by when?
  5. What must be communicated to Finance, principals, or district leadership?
