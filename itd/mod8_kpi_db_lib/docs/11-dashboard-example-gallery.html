Dashboard Example Gallery

Use these working examples to discuss what the dashboard is for, which KPIs belong on it, and what action the data should trigger. All numbers are illustrative Ardent ISD sample data.

GenAI data guardrail: Use fictional, public, aggregated, or properly sanitized information with public generative AI tools. Do not enter student records, staff records, personally identifiable information, credentials, sensitive cybersecurity details, legal records, confidential procurement material, vendor-confidential information, or sensitive district project information unless your district has explicitly approved the tool and data use.

1. Technology Operations Dashboard

Question: Are core technology services reliable enough to support district operations and instruction?

Includes network availability, critical application availability, device readiness, inventory accuracy, help desk SLA, account provisioning, backup recovery, and campus breakdowns.

Open live example | View data.md

2. Help Desk / Customer Service Dashboard

Question: Are users receiving timely, effective support, and where is service pressure concentrated?

Includes open backlog, median first response, median resolution, SLA compliance, customer satisfaction, backlog aging, category mix, and campus comparisons.

Open live example | View data.md

3. Device Refresh Project Dashboard

Question: Will the district complete device deployment on time, within budget, and with acceptable rollout quality?

Includes devices received, staged, deployed, campuses complete, schedule milestones, budget forecast, deployment defects, and unresolved logistics risks.

Open live example | View data.md

4. MFA Change-Adoption Dashboard

Question: Is MFA technically deployed and actually usable/sustainable for staff?

Includes enrollment, awareness, training, successful use, support demand, exceptions, campus champions, and 60-day reinforcement.

Open live example | View data.md

5. LMS Adoption Dashboard

Question: Is the LMS being used meaningfully and consistently across campuses, or are accounts merely active?

Includes weekly meaningful use, course activity, training, assignment/feedback workflows, student use, support demand, and campus adoption differences.

Open live example | View data.md

6. Project Portfolio Dashboard

Question: Which major technology initiative needs leadership attention right now?

Includes projects on track, on-time milestones, portfolio forecast variance, open high risks, adoption, and decisions needed.

Open live example | View data.md

7. Executive Technology Dashboard

Question: How is the technology function performing across reliability, service, projects, security readiness, finance, and adoption?

This is deliberately a high-level cross-domain dashboard suitable for cabinet discussion. Detailed operational or sensitive cybersecurity measures stay in internal scorecards rather than the executive/public layer.

Open live example | View data.md

Gallery Discussion Questions

For each example, ask:

  1. Who is the audience?
  2. What decision can this dashboard support?
  3. Which measures are outcomes, which are activities, and which are context?
  4. What information is missing before acting?
  5. What should remain internal or restricted?
  6. Which KPI could be removed with little loss of decision value?
  7. What should happen when a KPI moves from On Track to Watch or Critical?
