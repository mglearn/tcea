Cross‑curricular & skills

  • Critical thinking: a three‑step evidence routine — identify from clues, infer purpose/role, and connect to what followed — practiced across nine cases.
  • Reading & academic vocabulary: short informational text, domain terms, and a written reflection.
  • ELPS: seven languages, visual anchors, and repeated sentence frames.
