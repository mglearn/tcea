Turn Assessment Data Into Action

A turnkey workflow that takes you from a spreadsheet of student responses to a phase-tagged, ACE-recommended item analysis in about ten minutes — using any Gen AI chatbot you already have.

See the workflowGet the files

Facilitators: Diana Benner & Miguel Guhlin

What this is

Gen AI can analyze your assessment data, but only if you set it up for success the way you would a student. This site gives you a ready-made kit that works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or any free chatbot — no special tool, no subscription, no coding.

The idea

You bring three things to the chatbot: a context primer that teaches it the frameworks, a master prompt that tells it exactly what to do, and your CSV of student responses. It returns an item analysis, a phase of learning for each student, a SOLO level, and an ACE next step for each group. Then you run a one-paste audit to catch invented data before you trust it.

Why it works

Left alone, a chatbot jumps straight to activities — it has never seen your students. Two instructions fix that: ask clarifying questions first slows it down, and use only the sources I provide stops it from inventing effect sizes and fake research. The primer supplies the grounded content; the prompt enforces the discipline.

The three-step loop

1

Prime

Attach the context primer and paste the master prompt. The chatbot now knows SOLO, ACE, the phases of learning, and the effect-size table — and knows not to invent any of it.

2

Analyze

Attach your de-identified CSV. The chatbot returns item percentages, a phase and SOLO level per student, groupings, and an ACE next step per group.

3

Audit

Paste the audit checklist. The chatbot recomputes a column to prove its math, confirms every number came from your data, and checks that each strategy matches its phase.

Walk through it in detail
Privacy first. Never put student names into a public AI tool. The templates use student numbers. If you cannot de-identify, use the no-AI browser-based item analysis tool instead — no data leaves your computer.
