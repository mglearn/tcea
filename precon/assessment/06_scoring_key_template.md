# Scoring Key Template
# Fill this in for your own assessment, then include it with your CSV so the
# AI (or a colleague) knows which questions measure which phase.

## Assessment: _____________________________   Grade/Subject: ______________

| Question | Type            | Phase measured | Correct / proficient means...          |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|----------------------------------------|
| Q1       | Drawing         | Surface        | Shows multiple connected parts         |
| Q2       | Multiple choice | Surface        | Correct option selected                     |
| Q3       | Short answer    | Deep           | Explains a cause/relationship          |
| Q4       | Matching        | Surface        | All terms matched correctly            |
| Q5       | Comparison      | Transfer       | Detailed comparison with reasoning     |

## Phase thresholds (edit to fit your assessment)

- Surface: correct on 1-2 of the Surface items only.
- Deep: correct on all Surface items AND the Deep item(s).
- Transfer: all of the above AND the Transfer item, with reasoning.

## Notes
- Keep student identifiers as numbers or initials - never full names in an AI tool.
- If one item is much lower than the rest, treat it as a class-wide gap to teach,
  not as individual student failure.
