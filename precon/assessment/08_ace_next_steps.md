# ACE Next Steps - quick reference

ACE is built on the SOLO Taxonomy. It tells you the NEXT move for a student
based on the phase they are in now.

## Articulate It  (for Surface learners)
Goal: get foundational knowledge stated clearly and correctly.
Looks like:
- Vocabulary and definitions in the student's own words
- Labeling diagrams, restating steps, retrieval practice
- Sentence stems that force students to name what they know
Fitting strategies: Vocabulary Programs (0.62), Jigsaw (0.92, expert phase)

## Connect It  (moving Surface -> Deep)
Goal: link separate facts into relationships and explanations.
Looks like:
- Concept maps with labeled links ("causes", "leads to")
- Explaining how two ideas relate; compare/contrast
- Reciprocal Teaching roles: predict, question, clarify, summarize
Fitting strategies: Concept Mapping (0.66), Reciprocal Teaching (0.74),
Argumentation (0.86), Outlining & Organizing (0.85)

## Extend It  (moving Deep -> Transfer)
Goal: apply learning to a new, unfamiliar situation.
Looks like:
- Novel problems with multiple solution paths
- Designing, hypothesizing, defending a rationale
- Applying a principle across a different context or domain
Fitting strategies: Problem-Solving Teaching (0.61), Critical Thinking (0.79),
Transfer Strategies (0.75) - only after Surface + Deep are solid.
