Practice Scenarios

Ten classes to analyze yourself

Work these by hand first, then feed your own version to Gen AI. Ten scenarios span four grade bands and mix surface, deep, and transfer learning.

Download the spreadsheetScoring guide

How to use these

1

Open the spreadsheet

One tab per scenario, plus a Read Me. Each question column is tagged with the phase it measures.

2

Enter your data in the yellow cells

One row per student, 1 for correct/proficient or 0 for not yet. The % correct row and each student's phase estimate update as you type.

3

Export that tab to CSV

Then use it with the master prompt and context primer in the Gen AI portion of the session. The two analyses — yours and the AI's — are what you compare.

The spreadsheet estimates a phase for each student, but you decide. The estimate is a starting point from the question totals; a mixed pattern is a professional judgment call, and that judgment is the point of the exercise.
