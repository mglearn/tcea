# Practice Scenarios - Scoring Guide

For each scenario, mark every student 1 (correct/proficient) or 0 (not yet) on each question.
Each question is tagged by the phase of learning it measures. Use the phase rule at the bottom to decide each student's overall phase.

Phases: **surface** = facts, vocabulary, basic skills · **deep** = connecting and explaining ideas · **transfer** = applying to a new situation.

## Phase decision rule

- **Surface:** correct on most surface items (all but one), but not yet on deep or transfer.
- **Deep:** surface items secure AND correct on the deep item(s).
- **Transfer:** surface and deep secure AND correct on the transfer item.
- **Emerging:** surface items not yet secure - the student needs foundational support first.

The workbook auto-estimates this for you, but your professional judgment decides the final phase, especially on mixed patterns (watch for the understanding-vs-production gap: a student may know more than their writing shows).
