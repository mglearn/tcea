No-AI Item Analyzer

Analyze your CSV right here

Drop in a CSV and get item percentages, each student's phase of learning, and grouped next steps. Nothing is uploaded — the analysis runs entirely in your browser.

Private by design. Your file never leaves your device. This page does all the math locally in your browser, so it's safe for real student data. (Still, use student IDs, not names, as a good habit.)
Drop a CSV here, or click to choose
Exported from the scoring spreadsheet, or any CSV with a student_id column and question columns ending in _surface, _deep, or _transfer.

How it decides a phase

Same rules as the chatbot pack, applied consistently:

PhaseThe student…
surfaceis secure on the surface items but not yet the deep or transfer ones.
deepis secure on surface AND deep items.
transferis secure across surface, deep, AND the transfer item.
emerginghasn't secured the surface items yet — needs foundational support first.
This is a starting point, not a verdict. The estimate comes from the score pattern; your professional judgment decides the final phase, especially on mixed results. For a richer written report and follow-up prompts, use the chatbot pack instead.
