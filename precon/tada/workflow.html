The Turnkey Workflow

From CSV to next steps, in one sitting

Follow these six steps with any Gen AI chatbot. Everything you paste is in the downloadable pack.

Download the pack

Before you start

De-identify your data. Replace names with numbers (S01, S02…). Score each question 1 (correct) or 0 (not yet), and label each column with the phase it measures, e.g. Q3_deep. The template is already set up this way.

The six steps

1

Open any chatbot

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Z.ai, or Mistral. Free tiers work. Start a fresh chat so nothing else confuses it.

2

Prime it with the context file

Attach 01_context_primer.md, or paste its contents. This gives the AI the phases of learning, SOLO levels, the ACE framework, and the effect-size table — and tells it to use only those, never invented numbers.

3

Paste the master prompt

Paste 03_master_prompt.md. It instructs the AI to run the item analysis, assign a phase and SOLO level per student, group them, and give each group an ACE next step — and to ask you questions first if anything is unclear.

4

Attach your CSV

Attach your data, or practice with 04_sample_class_data.csv first. Not sure how to export? See 07_export_csv_howto.md for Google, Excel, Canvas, and Schoology.

5

Read the analysis

You get item percentages, a phase per student with one-line reasoning, phase groupings, and an ACE recommendation plus a matched high-effect-size strategy for each group — then a short "what to do tomorrow."

6

Audit before you trust it

Paste 05_audit_checklist.md. The AI recomputes a column to prove its math, confirms every figure came from your data, and verifies no Transfer strategy was handed to a Surface group. It lists whatever it corrected.

What the output looks like

Run against the 10-student sample set, the item analysis alone already tells a story: the class is solid on matching and basic recall, but the transfer question collapses.

The teaching move writes itself: most students are secure at Surface but stall at the Surface→Deep connection (Q3). That is a Connect It group — reach for Concept Mapping (0.66) or Reciprocal Teaching (0.74), not a transfer task yet.

Why the audit matters

The single most common Gen AI failure in this task is confidently reporting percentages that don't match your file, or citing an effect size it made up. Step 6 forces the model to show its arithmetic and trace every claim back to the primer. It takes one paste and turns a plausible-looking answer into a checked one.
