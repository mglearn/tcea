Session Overview

Turn Assessment Data Into Action

A three-hour, hands-on workshop: learn the frameworks, apply them by hand to real data, then validate your judgment with Gen AI.

Facilitators: Diana Benner & Miguel Guhlin

What you'll do

Formative assessment can double student learning gains, but only if the data changes what you do next. You'll learn to identify where each student is in their learning (surface, deep, transfer), match the right high-effect-size strategy to each phase, and work a realistic scenario — five students, a pre-assessment, a score sheet — by hand. Then you'll bring in Gen AI to validate your thinking, run the same item analysis in seconds, and compare your judgment against the AI's. You'll leave able to set up any chatbot for reliable results, export your gradebook data as a CSV, and move from raw scores to a concrete instructional plan.

Agenda

Entry Ticket · 5 min · 0:00–0:05

Quick poll: "How confident are you turning assessment data into next-day instruction?" Sets the baseline.

Block 1 · Intro to the Frameworks · 40 min · 0:05–0:45

  • Phases of learning: surface, deep, transfer
  • SOLO Taxonomy and the ACE Framework (Articulate, Connect, Extend)
  • High-Effect-Size Instructional Strategies matched to phases: Jigsaw (surface/deep), Argumentation, Outlining/Organizing, Reciprocal Teaching (deep), Service Learning and Problem-Solving Teaching (transfer)
  • Opening activity: Jigsaw — small groups each take one phase/strategy pairing and teach it back

Block 2 · Apply It by Hand · 50 min · 0:45–1:35

  • The scenarios: ten editable classes across K-2, 3-5, 6-8, and 9-12 (see the Scenarios page) — each mixing surface, deep, and transfer items
  • Work the data to identify each student's phase of learning
  • Plan interventions and differentiation for each

Break · 10 min · 1:35–1:45

Block 3 · Validate with Gen AI · 55 min · 1:45–2:40

  • Set up Gen AI for success (reliable, grounded results)
  • Export your data as a CSV file
  • Run the item analysis with Gen AI
  • Verify and validate: compare your by-hand analysis against the AI's assessment
  • Where the two agree, where they differ, and which to trust

Block 4 · From Analysis to Teaching · 15 min · 2:40–2:55

  • Share out about the data and the process
  • Modify teaching to match each student's phase of learning
  • Debrief across groups

Exit Ticket · 5 min · 2:55–3:00

"What's the first thing you'll try, and what's one question you still have?"
