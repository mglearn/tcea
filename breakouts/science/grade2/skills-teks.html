Skills & TEKS Alignment

For the featured breakout Make Some Noise! and the Grade 2 idea set — aligned to the 2021 Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Science (§112.4). Pitched to early readers: observe, sort, sequence, and name.

🎯 Ideas to build (and fix)

🚨 Common early ideas this breakout gently corrects: “a still object can make a sound on its own” (sound needs something vibrating); and “sound just appears at your ear” (vibrations travel through the air to get there).

📋 Lock-by-lock alignment

Recurring themes: patterns, cause & effect, forms of energy, systems.
