Word Bank · Grade 2

Science Words to Know

Big science words for Grade 2 — in easy words, with an example. Keep this open while you play!

🔊 Sound

sound
Something you hear.Like: a drum beat.
vibrate
To move back and forth very fast.Like: a guitar string vibrates to make a sound.

🧊 Matter

solid
Matter that keeps its own shape.Like: a block or a rock.
liquid
Matter that flows and takes the shape of its cup.Like: water or juice.
melt
When a solid gets warm and turns into a liquid.Like: ice melts.
freeze
When a liquid gets cold and turns into a solid.Like: water freezes into ice.

👋 Forces

push
A force that moves something away.Like: pushing a swing.
collide
When two things bump into each other.Like: a ball hits a wall.

☀️ Sky & Weather

star
A giant ball of light in space. The Sun is a star.Like: the Sun gives us light.
reflect
To bounce light back. The Moon reflects the Sun's light.Like: the Moon glows at night.
thermometer
A tool that tells how hot or cold it is.Like: it reads the temperature.
severe weather
Wild, strong weather.Like: a hurricane, tornado, or flood.
recycle
To turn old paper, plastic, or metal into something new.Like: an old can becomes a new can.

🌱 Living Things

producer
A living thing that makes its own food.Like: a plant using sunlight.
consumer
A living thing that eats other living things.Like: a rabbit eating a plant.
pollen
Yellow dust that bees move from flower to flower to help plants make seeds.Like: a bee carries pollen.
metamorphosis
When an animal changes shape a lot as it grows.Like: a caterpillar becomes a butterfly.
