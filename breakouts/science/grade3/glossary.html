Science Words to Know

Key science words for Grade 3 — in plain words, with an example. Keep this open while you play!

🚀 Space

orbit
To travel around something in a path.Like: Earth orbits the Sun.
solar system
The Sun and everything that orbits it, like the planets and moons.Like: Earth is in our solar system.

🧊 Matter

solid
Matter with a definite shape of its own.Like: a block or ice.
liquid
Matter that takes the shape of its container.Like: water in a cup.
gas
Matter that spreads to fill its whole container.Like: air in a balloon.
evaporate
When a liquid turns into a gas.Like: a puddle drying up.
condensation
When a gas cools into a liquid.Like: drops on a cold glass.
mass
How much matter is in an object.Like: measured on a scale.

⚡ Forces & Energy

gravity
A force that pulls things down toward Earth, without touching them.Like: a dropped ball falls.
magnetism
A force that pulls some metals, even from a distance.Like: a magnet grabs a paper clip.
mechanical energy
The energy of a moving object.Like: a rolling ball.

🌍 Earth

weathering
When wind, water, and freezing break rock into tiny bits.Like: rock slowly wearing away.
soil
Weathered rock mixed with rotted plants and animals.Like: the dirt plants grow in.
earthquake
A sudden shaking that can crack the ground quickly.Like: a rapid Earth change.

🌱 Living Things

food chain
How energy flows from the Sun to plants to animals.Like: sun → grass → bug → frog.
fossil
The preserved remains or print of a living thing from long ago.Like: a dinosaur footprint in rock.
structure
A body part that helps an animal survive.Like: a duck's webbed feet.
