Skills & TEKS Alignment

For the featured breakout Launch Control — aligned to the 2021 Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Science (§112.28).

🎯 Misconceptions confronted

🚨 “mass disappears when fuel burns” (atoms only rearrange — in a sealed system mass is conserved); “a heavier rocket accelerates faster because it's bigger” (for a fixed force, more mass means less acceleration); and “amplitude and wavelength are the same thing” (amplitude is energy; wavelength is the crest-to-crest distance).

📋 Lock-by-lock alignment

Recurring themes: cause & effect, systems & models, energy & matter, scale/proportion/quantity, stability & change.
