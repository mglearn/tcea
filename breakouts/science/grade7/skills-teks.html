🎯 Misconceptions confronted

🚨 “a flat line on a distance–time graph means moving at a steady speed” (it means stopped); “speed and velocity are the same” (velocity adds direction and depends on displacement); “a downhill line means slowing down” (it means returning to the start); and “insulation adds cold / stops heat” (it only slows heat, which always flows hot→cold to equilibrium).
