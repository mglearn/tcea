Science Words to Know

Key science words for Grade 4 — in plain words, with an example. Keep this open while you play!

⚡ Energy & Electricity

circuit
A closed loop that electricity flows around.Like: battery → wire → bulb → back.
conductor
A material that lets heat and electricity pass through easily.Like: metal.
insulator
A material that blocks or slows heat and electricity.Like: plastic, rubber, wood.
energy transfer
When energy moves from one object to another.Like: a ball knocking down pins.

🧪 Matter

solution
A mixture where one thing dissolves evenly into another.Like: salt in water.
conserved
Stays the same. When things mix, the total matter is conserved.Like: mass adds up the same.

🌍 Earth & Space

phases
The changing shapes of the Moon we see through the month.Like: new moon to full moon.
evaporation
When the Sun heats water and it rises into the air as a gas.Like: a puddle drying up.
water cycle
How water moves around Earth: evaporation, clouds, rain, and back.Like: powered by the Sun.
erosion
When moving water or wind carries away bits of rock and soil.Like: a river carving a canyon.
climate
The usual weather of a place over many years.Like: a desert is usually dry.
renewable
A resource that won't run out soon.Like: sunlight or wind.
nonrenewable
A resource that can run out.Like: coal or oil.

🌱 Living Things

decomposer
A living thing that breaks down dead things and recycles nutrients.Like: a mushroom.
inherited trait
A trait passed from parents that you are born with.Like: eye color.
acquired trait
A trait gained during life, not from parents.Like: learning to ride a bike.
