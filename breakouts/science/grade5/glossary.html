Science Words to Know

Key science words for Grade 5 — in plain words, with an example. Keep this open while you play!

🌍 Earth & Space

rotate
To spin on an axis. Earth rotates once about every 24 hours.Like: Earth's spin makes day and night.
shadow
A dark shape made when an object blocks light.Like: long in the morning, short at midday.
sedimentary rock
Rock made from pressed and cemented layers of sediment.Like: the oldest layers are on the bottom.
fossil fuels
Coal, oil, and gas made from ancient buried living things.Like: they took millions of years to form.
landform
A shape of the land made by wind, water, or ice.Like: a canyon, delta, or dune.

🧪 Matter

particles
The tiny bits that all matter is made of, too small to see.Like: air is made of particles.
mixture
Two or more things mixed but not joined; the parts keep their properties.Like: iron filings in sand.

⚡ Force & Energy

force
A push or pull that can change how something moves.Like: an unequal force wins a tug-of-war.
energy transformation
When energy changes from one form to another.Like: chemical → electrical → light in a flashlight.
reflection
When light bounces off a surface.Like: light off a mirror.
refraction
When light bends passing into water or a lens.Like: a straw looks bent in water.

🌱 Living Things

ecosystem
A web of living and nonliving things that are all connected.Like: a change ripples through the whole web.
pollution
Trash or dirty water and air that harm an ecosystem.Like: littering in a stream.
instinct
A behavior an animal is born knowing.Like: baby turtles heading to the sea.
learned behavior
A behavior an animal gains from experience.Like: orcas learning to hunt in packs.
