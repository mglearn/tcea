Skills & TEKS Alignment

For the featured breakout Push, Pull, and Play! and the Grade 1 idea set — aligned to the 2021 Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Science (§112.3). Pitched to early readers: observe, sort, sequence, and name.

🎯 Ideas to build (and fix)

🚨 Common early ideas this breakout gently corrects: “things move by themselves” (a push or a pull is needed to start motion); “you must touch something to move it” (a magnet can pull without touching); and “a pull and a push are the same” (a pull comes toward you, a push goes away).

📋 Lock-by-lock alignment

Recurring themes: patterns, cause & effect, properties, systems (parts of a whole).
