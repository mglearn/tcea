Engineering Design Challenge · Grade 1

Roll It Far!

A hands-on STEM buddy for the Push, Pull, and Play! breakout. Build a ramp and use a push to send a toy car as far as you can — then make it even better.

🎯 The problem

Make a ramp that helps a toy car roll far. Let the car go from the top and see how far it rolls. Then change your ramp to make the car go even farther!

Big question: Does a taller (steeper) ramp make the car roll farther than a low ramp?

🧰 What you need

  • A toy car or a ball that rolls
  • Something to make a ramp: a book, a board, or a piece of cardboard
  • Blocks or books to lift the top of the ramp up
  • A way to measure: floor tiles, big steps, or a measuring tape

🔁 Be an engineer

  1. Ask: How can I make the car roll far?
  2. Imagine: Should the ramp be high or low? Take a guess!
  3. Plan: Pick your ramp and how high to lift it.
  4. Build: Set up your ramp. Let the car go from the top (no pushing — let gravity pull it!).
  5. Test: Measure how far the car rolled. Write it down.
  6. Make it better: Change one thing — make the ramp higher — and test again.
