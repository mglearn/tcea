Science Words to Know

Big science words for Grade 1 — in easy words, with a picture example. Keep this open while you play!

👋 Push & Pull

push
To move something away from you.Like: you push a swing.
pull
To move something toward you.Like: you pull a wagon.
force
A push or a pull that makes something move.Like: a kick moves a ball.
motion
When something is moving.Like: a rolling ball is in motion.
magnet
A thing that can pull metal, even without touching it.Like: a magnet grabs paper clips.

🔥 Hot & Cold

heat
Warm energy that can change things.Like: heat cooks food.
melt
When something warm turns from solid to liquid.Like: ice melts into water.
freeze
When something cold turns to ice.Like: water freezes in winter.

🌍 Earth & Sky

season
One of the four parts of the year: spring, summer, fall, winter.Like: summer is hot.
weather
What the sky and air are doing today.Like: it is rainy.
soil
The dirt on the ground that plants grow in.Like: seeds grow in soil.
ocean
The biggest water on Earth. It is salty.Like: fish swim in the ocean.
save (conserve)
To use only the water you need and not waste it.Like: turn the faucet off.

🌱 Living Things

living
A thing that grows, needs food and water, and can have babies.Like: a dog is living.
nonliving
A thing that does not grow or eat.Like: a rock is nonliving.
food chain
A picture of who eats what.Like: sun → grass → bug → bird.
life cycle
How a living thing grows and changes.Like: egg → chick → hen.
parent
A grown animal that has babies. Babies look like their parents.Like: a puppy looks like its dog parent.
