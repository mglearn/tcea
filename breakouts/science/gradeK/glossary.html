Science Words to Know

Big science words for Kindergarten — in easy words, with a picture example. Keep this open while you play!

🔦 Light & Sky

light
What helps us see. It comes from the Sun or a lamp.Like: we need light to see.
shadow
A dark shape made when something blocks the light.Like: your hand shadow on the wall.
day
The light time when the Sun is up.Like: we play in the day.
night
The dark time when we see the Moon and stars.Like: we sleep at night.
clouds
White, fluffy things in the sky. Dark clouds bring rain.Like: a rainy day has clouds.

🌍 Earth & Weather

rock
A hard piece of the earth on the ground.Like: sort rocks by size.
weather
What the sky is doing today.Like: sunny, rainy, or windy.
wind
Moving air we can feel.Like: wind flies a kite.
air
What is all around us, even though we can't see it.Like: air fills a balloon.

👋 Forces

push
To move something away from you.Like: push a toy car.
pull
To move something toward you. A magnet gives a pull.Like: a magnet pulls a clip.
magnet
A thing that pulls some metal.Like: it grabs a paper clip.

🌱 Living Things

seed
A tiny thing that grows into a plant.Like: a seed grows a flower.
roots
Plant parts that grow down and drink water.Like: roots hold the plant.
shelter
An animal's safe home.Like: a nest or a den.
