Skills & TEKS Alignment

For the featured breakout Light and Shadows and the Kindergarten idea set — aligned to the 2021 Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Science (§112.2). Pitched to our youngest learners: observe, sort, sequence, and name.

🎯 Ideas to build

🚨 Early ideas this breakout builds: we can only see things when there is light; and when something blocks the light, it makes a shadow. (A common early idea to gently correct: “we can see in the dark” — we need a light source to see.)
