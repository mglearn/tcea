Bible as Literature

A TCEA Critical Thinking Online Breakout · Miguel Guhlin

Texas adopted Bible stories for K–12 classrooms. This free suite studies those exact texts the way courts and the TEA say they must be studied — academically, as story and history, never devotionally. Students open clues, weigh evidence, separate what a text says from what a reader believes, and crack the locks.
