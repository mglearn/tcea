Privacy & Compliance

These breakouts are built to be safe for every classroom in Texas and beyond. They collect nothing, require no accounts, and run entirely in the student's browser.

What we collect: nothing

  • No accounts, no logins, no sign-ups.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or advertising.
  • All activity state (which locks are open) lives only in the browser during the session and disappears when the tab closes.
  • The only outside request is to Google Fonts for typefaces. Self-host the two font files to reach zero external egress.
  • Every page sets referrer: no-referrer.

Content framing

This is a study resource about religious literature — not religious instruction. The Bible stories here are the texts Texas adopted for K–12 classrooms (Bluebonnet Learning and the 2025 required reading list), and they are taught academically: analyzing story, genre, theme, archetype, and allusion. There is no devotional content, prayer, or truth claim. This approach follows the line drawn in Abington School District v. Schempp (1963) and stated by the TEA for its own materials.

A note for families and teachers

Because these texts are religiously significant to many families and not to others, the activities are written to include every student — those of any faith and those of none. Where the adopted materials and scholars disagree about a story's meaning, students are taught to notice the disagreement, not to take a side.

Teacher answer keys are protected with client-side AES-256 encryption; the password is never transmitted. This is appropriate for answer keys but is not a substitute for server-side protection of any paid or sensitive materials.
