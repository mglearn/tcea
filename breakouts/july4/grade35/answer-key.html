🇺🇸 The Flag Maker's Workshop

    Answer: 13

  3. Lock 3 — Word Lock [word]
    The colors clue says the flag's NICKNAME is the "Stars and _____." Type the missing word.

    Why: The maker's note says blue first, then red, then white. Following the given order — instead of your favorite color — is the careful-thinking move.
