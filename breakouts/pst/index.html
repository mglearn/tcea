Problem-Solving Teaching

Ready-to-teach Problem-Based Learning (PBL) units for the Texas history and social studies classroom. These are not breakouts — students work a real, ill-structured problem as a stakeholder while the teacher guides. Every unit builds deliberately from surface → deep → transfer learning, because problem-solving is where knowledge transfers and accelerates achievement. Choose a grade to begin.

① Surface

Build the knowledge

Vocabulary, key facts, and a jigsaw read — the raw material students will reason with.

② Deep

Connect & organize

Concept maps, primary sources, points of view, and argument — relating ideas to each other.

③ Transfer

Solve the problem

The PBL scenario: take a role, ask questions, investigate, propose a solution, and debrief.
