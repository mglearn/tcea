Search by idiom, theme, language, or grade — or pick a grade band. (More breakouts are on the way; this is the Phase 1 English + Spanish start.)

How it's taught. Idioms are Tier-1 vocabulary that trip up multilingual learners because the words don't add up to the meaning. Each breakout separates the literal picture from the real meaning, uses context, and compares the idiom across languages — sometimes the same image (hand ↔ mano), sometimes a different one (arm & leg ↔ un ojo). Home languages are welcomed as a resource, aligned to the Texas ELPS (idiomatic expressions) and WIDA figurative-language expectations. See the TEKS / ELPS alignment & appropriateness guide →
