The Method

The VIBES Framework

Every prompt below includes a VIBES block tuned to that build. Five steps, one for each letter — expand any prompt's block to see it.

V
Vision

Decide what you want and who it's for before you open a chat window.

I
Instruct

Give the AI your colors, your data, your constraints, and where it will live.

B
Build

Generate in small chunks and iterate conversationally, one feature at a time.

E
Evaluate

Run it, read it, click everything on a phone and a laptop. Verify links and facts.

S
Ship

Host on GitHub Pages, embed in Google Sites, or deploy as an Apps Script web app.

Paste · Iterate · Ship

The LibVibes Prompt Library

All 18 prompts. Hit Copy prompt, paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or BoodleBox, then iterate with the expandable VIBES plan. Each prompt also has its own shareable page — click a title or ↗ Open page to link straight to a single starter. The four originals on the main page (ShareSpace, WonderWall, StickyBoard, Book Recommendation Gallery) stay exactly as they are — these fill the gaps around them.
