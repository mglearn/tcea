How the operators work (tap to open)
  • sharks AND ocean — both words must appear. Narrows results.
  • sharks OR whales — either word. Widens results.
  • sharks NOT attack — has "sharks" but excludes "attack".
  • "climate change" — the exact phrase, in that order.
  • (sharks OR whales) AND ocean — parentheses group ideas together.
