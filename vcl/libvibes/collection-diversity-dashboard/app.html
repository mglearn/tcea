Weeding cutoffs

A title is flagged as a weeding candidate if its copyright year is at or before the year cutoff and its last checkout is before the date cutoff (or it has never circulated). CREW/MUSTIE-style — verify against your own judgment.

Titles by Genre

Where your collection is concentrated. Thin genres may be growth areas; very tall bars may be over-collected.

Titles by Format

Print vs. digital vs. audio balance. Useful when planning what to buy next.

Titles by Copyright Decade

Tall bars on the left mean an aging collection. A long tail in older decades is a signal to review for currency and accuracy.

Representation Tags

A quick scan of the diversity tags you've added. Small numbers next to important themes can point to collection gaps worth filling.
