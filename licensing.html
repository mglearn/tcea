What ShareAlike means

If you adapt these materials, your version must carry the same license. Remix a checklist, translate a guide, or rebuild a workshop around this material, and what you publish must also be CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — free for others to use noncommercially, credited, and equally open to further adaptation.

You may not add restrictions, lock an adaptation behind a paywall, or release it under terms that give your users less freedom than you received. Using the material unchanged carries no ShareAlike obligation — it applies only when you publish something derived from it.

How to credit

Attribution is required under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Include the material's name, the copyright holder, the creator, and the license:

[Material name] © 2026 TCEA, created by Miguel Guhlin. Licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

If you adapted it, say so — for example, "Adapted from [Material name] © 2026 TCEA, created by Miguel Guhlin. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0." Where practical, link back to this directory so others can find the original.

Provenance

This directory collects the working materials behind TCEA professional learning: AI adoption checklists and roadmaps, prompt libraries, coaching and rubric tools, accessibility and instructional-design guides, breakout and critical-thinking activities, and session companions for conference presentations and workshops.

Materials are produced with the help of generative-AI tools and refined under Miguel Guhlin's direction and editorial review; a human stays in the loop on every decision. Where a page draws on external frameworks, standards, or published research, those sources are cited on the page itself and remain the property of their respective owners.

Privacy

These pages run in the browser. Tools that save your work keep it on your own device using browser storage; nothing is uploaded and no personal data is collected or transmitted unless a page explicitly asks you to submit something and says so at the point of asking.
