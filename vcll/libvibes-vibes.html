The Method

The VIBES Framework

A five-step loop for vibe-coding any of these tools — the shared mental model behind every prompt in the library.

V
Vision

Decide what you want and who it's for before you open a chat window.

I
Instruct

Give the AI your colors, your data, your constraints, and where it will live.

B
Build

Generate in small chunks and iterate conversationally, one feature at a time.

E
Evaluate

Run it, read it, click everything on a phone and a laptop. Verify links and facts.

S
Ship

Host on GitHub Pages, embed in Google Sites, or deploy as an Apps Script web app.

What each step really means

  • VVision. Finish this sentence before you type a word to the AI: “This is for ___ so they can ___.” One audience, one job. A sharp vision is also your finish line — it tells you when you're done.
  • IInstruct. Whatever the AI doesn't know, it invents — fake hours, generic colors, placeholder books. Feed it the real specifics: your data, your campus colors, and the one constraint that matters most (“no logins,” “one HTML file”).
  • BBuild. Get the skeleton working first, confirm it runs, then add one feature per turn. Small steps mean that when something breaks, you know exactly which change caused it.
  • EEvaluate. Looking right is not the same as working. Click every button, test on a real phone, feed it weird input, and verify any facts (citations, Dewey ranges) against a real source.
  • SShip. A tool nobody can find helps nobody. Put the working result where your people already are — a GitHub Pages link, a Google Sites embed, a QR code at the shelf, a loop on the lobby monitor.
VIBES is a loop, not a staircase. You'll cycle Build → Evaluate → Build several times before you Ship — that's the process working. The AI's first answer is a starting point, never the finish line. Each prompt in the library includes a VIBES block tuned to that specific build.
