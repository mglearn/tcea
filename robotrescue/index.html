Mission Briefing

The sheepdogs are out sick. Your robot team has been called in!

  1. Choose a robot attachment.
  2. Add movement commands to your program.
  3. Select Run Program and watch what happens.
  4. Revise your attachment or code, then try again.
  5. Herd as many sheep as possible through the green gate.
Engineer’s rule: A program may use up to 30 commands. Turns rotate the robot in place. A move that would leave the field or hit a fence is skipped.
