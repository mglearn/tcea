Vendor comparison

Teacher Observation and Coaching Platforms

Review one platform at a time. The slides distinguish formal evaluation workflow tools from coaching, reflection, and teacher-preparation products.

1

Observation workflow and video coaching

Sibme

Combines recorded lessons, synced notes, transcripts, artifacts, rubric alignment, and AI-assisted analysis. Sibme specifically markets support for T-TESS and customizable district observation frameworks.

Visit Sibme ↗

Best fit

A connected formal observation and coaching process

Strong candidate when a district wants evidence capture, feedback, video coaching, and framework-aligned documentation in one system.

Verify before purchase Confirm the exact T-TESS configuration, evaluator controls, AI evidence citations, retention rules, and whether coaching records can remain separate from appraisal records.

2

AI-assisted feedback drafting

Edthena Observation Copilot

Turns an administrator's walkthrough or observation notes into editable, framework-aligned feedback, including suggested strengths, questions, and next steps.

Visit Edthena ↗

Best fit

Reducing post-observation writing time

Useful when observers already collect solid evidence but lose time organizing notes and composing feedback afterward.

Verify before purchase Test whether generated comments accurately distinguish evidence from interpretation and match your district's T-TESS language without overstating performance.

3

Collaborative video coaching

Edthena VC3

Teachers record or upload lessons, share them securely, and receive timestamped feedback. Built-in insights can summarize lessons, surface coaching questions, and show student-to-teacher talk-time balance.

Explore VC3 ↗

Best fit

Instructional coaching, induction, and peer observation

Better suited to developmental coaching and collaborative reflection than to managing an entire formal T-TESS appraisal cycle.

Verify before purchase Decide who controls video sharing, how long recordings remain available, and whether formal evaluators can access coaching-only conversations.

4

Self-guided professional learning

Edthena AI Coach

Guides teachers through private self-observation, goal setting, reflection, and action planning. It is designed to extend coaching capacity between human coaching sessions.

Explore AI Coach ↗

Best fit

Low-stakes teacher reflection and individual growth

Useful as a professional learning support where teachers need frequent reflection opportunities without waiting for an administrator or coach.

Verify before purchase Keep self-guided coaching data separate from high-stakes evaluation unless district policy, contracts, and staff expectations explicitly allow that use.

5

AI-supported video feedback

Vosaic

Provides browser-based lesson recording, video tagging, clip-based comments, automated transcription, talk-time analysis, highlight reels, and AI-assisted feedback.

Visit Vosaic ↗

Best fit

Evidence-rich coaching and remote observation

Strong for reflective coaching, educator preparation, and programs that need precise comments tied to recorded classroom moments.

Verify before purchase Confirm how district rubrics are configured and whether the platform can manage every required step of a formal T-TESS process, not only video review.

6

Districtwide evaluation management

Frontline Professional Growth

Manages configurable rubrics, evaluation forms, observations, goals, student learning objectives, reports, professional learning, and records for teachers and other employee groups.

Visit Frontline ↗

Best fit

Managing the complete district evaluation workflow

Strong option when HR, professional learning, evaluation forms, approvals, and records must live in one district system.

Verify before purchase Its public materials emphasize workflow management more than AI analysis of classroom video. Confirm any Gen AI features needed for evidence analysis or feedback drafting.

7

Private instructional audio analysis

TeachFX

Records classroom audio and gives teachers private feedback on talk ratio, questioning, student participation, and instructional patterns. TeachFX states that the product is designed to support teachers, not evaluate them.

Visit TeachFX ↗

Best fit

Student-talk goals and frequent private reflection

Useful for coaching around discussion, wait time, questioning, and student voice, especially when video is unnecessary.

Verify before purchase Do not assume teacher-level audio analytics belong in formal appraisal. Establish clear boundaries around privacy, access, aggregation, and evaluator use.

8

Structured coaching cycles

TORSH Talent

Organizes observations, goals, comments, video feedback, professional learning resources, and data dashboards. Talent-Ed AI adds video auto-tagging, observation summaries, coaching insights, and enhanced search.

Visit TORSH ↗

Best fit

Scaling coaching across schools, regions, or programs

Strong for districts, service centers, universities, and programs that need consistent observation, feedback, reflection, and growth tracking.

Verify before purchase Ask for a live demonstration using your T-TESS rubric and required forms. Coaching functionality does not automatically equal full state appraisal compliance.

9

Teacher preparation and video assessment

GoReact

Supports recorded or live observations, time-coded text, audio, and video feedback, peer review, rubrics, searchable transcripts, and AI-assisted initial insights.

Visit GoReact ↗

Best fit

Teacher candidates, induction, and skills-based feedback

Particularly useful in educator preparation, field supervision, and induction programs where remote observation can reduce travel and scheduling problems.

Verify before purchase Confirm whether district appraisal records, signatures, summative forms, and T-TESS reporting can be managed without a second evaluation system.

10

Classroom observation and coaching management

Brings together classroom observations, real-time and timestamped feedback, action steps, meetings, lesson plans, forms, rubrics, video sharing, and observation scheduling.

Visit SchoolMint ↗

A customizable coaching and observation workspace

Worth comparing when a district wants observation forms, feedback, action steps, calendars, and video coaching in a shared system.
